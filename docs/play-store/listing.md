# Store listing texts

Copy each block into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main
store listing** (default language: English, United States). The length
limits are checked by `test/play_store_test.dart`.

## App name

Limit: 30 characters.

```text
Upwiq: Trading for Beginners
```

Alternatives if you prefer: `Upwiq: Learn to Read Markets`,
`Upwiq: Learn Trading Charts`.

## Short description

Limit: 80 characters.

```text
Learn to read charts with short lessons and risk-free practice trades.
```

## Full description

Limit: 4000 characters.

```text
Upwiq teaches you to read price charts from zero. Short lessons, charts you can touch, and practice trades with virtual money, so you can learn how markets work without risking a cent.

What works on one chart works on all of them. Upwiq teaches each idea once (candles, trends, support and resistance, risk) and shows it on stocks, forex, crypto, commodities and indices.

LEARN BY DOING
• 28 free bite-sized lessons: Market Foundations, Market Structure, Risk Management and the first Trading Psychology lessons
• Every lesson puts you on a chart: tap the level, find the swing, answer a quick question
• Plain language and clear illustrations, no jargon left unexplained
• Go at your own pace; your progress and XP are saved on your phone

PRACTISE WITHOUT RISK
• Place practice trades with an entry, a stop-loss and a target
• Learn position sizing: decide how much to risk before thinking about how much to make
• Realistic details such as the spread, so practice feels like the real thing
• Virtual money only: nothing to deposit, nothing to buy, nothing to lose

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
• The Daily Challenge: read a chart, size a position, plan a trade
• Build a streak and beat your best score
• Optional reminder, one a day at most

THE LEAGUE
• Pick a market, read the day's simulated news and trade the session with a virtual account
• Climb weekly leagues on a leaderboard of computer rivals
• The rules reward steady decisions over lucky bets: every trade needs a stop-loss and a sensible size

YOUR PRIVACY
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers
• Everything you do stays on your phone

MADE FOR BEGINNERS
Never looked at a chart before? Start with "What a market is" and build up one idea at a time. Dark and light themes included.

IMPORTANT
Upwiq is an educational app. It does not provide financial advice or recommendations, and it does not offer real trading. All trades are simulated with virtual money that cannot be bought, withdrawn or exchanged for anything of value. Results in a simulation do not reflect real trading, where costs, slippage and emotions differ. Trading involves risk of loss.
```
