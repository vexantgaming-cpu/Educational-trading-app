Upwiq is a beginner-friendly app that teaches you to read price charts: stocks, forex, crypto, commodities and indices. Short lessons, charts you can touch, and practice trades with virtual money.

  • Practise without risk

What Upwiq is not

Upwiq is not a broker and does not handle real money. It gives no trading signals and no personal recommendations. It is a place to learn how markets are read and to practise safely.

Your privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics. What you do in the app stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Upwiq is an educational app. It does not provide financial advice or recommendations, and it does not offer real trading. All trades are simulated with virtual money that cannot be bought or withdrawn. Trading involves risk of loss.
