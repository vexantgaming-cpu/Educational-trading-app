Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

  • Upwiq has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Everything you do in the app is saved on your phone only. We never receive it.
  • The Android app does not ask for permission to use the internet.
  • We only learn something about you if you choose to email us.

Who is responsible

Upwiq is made and published by an independent developer under the name Upwiq ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the little personal data described here. You can reach us at upwiqapp@gmail.com.

What the app saves on your phone

To remember where you are, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • Learning progress: which lessons you have finished and your XP.
  • Practice and the League: your virtual balance, league and results, and a ranked session you have paused.
  • Daily Challenge: your scores and streak.
  • Settings: dark or light theme, the daily reminder (on or off, and its time), and whether you have seen the welcome screens.

This information is not linked to your name, your email address or any identifier. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. The other players on the League leaderboard are simulated; your results are not shared with real people.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files. It does not read an advertising ID or other device identifiers, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software from other companies.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and books one reminder a day on your phone. Reminders are created by your phone itself; no message is sent from a server. You can switch them off in the app (Account, Daily reminder) or in your phone's settings at any time.

If you send us feedback

"Leave feedback" in the app prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you press send there. If you do, we receive your email address, your message, the rating and topic you chose and, if you leave "Include app details" switched on, the app version and the kind of device system (for example "android").

We use this only to reply to you and to improve the app. Under EU and UK data protection law, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages and improving Upwiq. Messages are kept in our mailbox, which is provided by Google (Gmail) and may be processed outside your country. We keep them for as long as needed to deal with your message and no longer than two years, and we delete them sooner if you ask.

Backups and Google Play

  • Phone backup. If backup is switched on in your Android settings, your phone may include the app's saved data in your own Google backup, so your progress can return on a new phone. That backup is between you and Google; we cannot see it.
  • Google Play. Google Play gives app publishers statistics such as the number of installs, ratings, and crash and performance reports from people who have agreed to share diagnostics with Google. These reports do not tell us who you are. Google's own privacy policy applies to how Google handles that data.

Purchases

Upwiq is free and has nothing to buy today. Virtual money in the app has no real value and cannot be bought or withdrawn. If a paid plan is added later, payment will be handled by Google Play and this policy will be updated first.

Children

Upwiq is made for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app's data: open Android Settings, Apps, Upwiq, Storage, and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Because the data only exists on your phone, this deletes it.
  • Emails you sent us: you can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to upwiqapp@gmail.com.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • United States: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts to collect data, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy: upwiqapp@gmail.com.

Upwiq is an educational app. It does not provide financial advice or recommendations, and it does not offer real trading. All trades are simulated with virtual money that cannot be bought or withdrawn. Trading involves risk of loss.
